ErrorRelay helps you report errors in AI products and developer tools without guessing what to write.

Give it a screenshot or the error text. ErrorRelay records when it happened (with timezone), figures out which product it is (and asks when unsure), turns your plain-language description into reproduction steps, drafts Expected / Actual behaviour, collects the environment details you can share, and scans everything for emails, phone numbers, API keys, tokens and passwords before anything leaves your device.

It then checks the official reporting channel for that product and tells you honestly how it can be delivered: some channels accept direct submission; OpenAI Support and Codex feedback are handed off to you with a prepared, copy-ready report. ErrorRelay never sends anything without your explicit confirmation and never claims a report was submitted unless the official channel returned a receipt.

Reports are stored in your own tenant, encrypted at rest, deleted automatically after the retention period, and can be exported as TXT, Markdown, JSON or a ZIP package at any time.

ErrorRelay is an independent tool and is not affiliated with OpenAI.
